EVERYTHING ON PROPERTY

The club amenities.

EASY PACE. ELEVATED TASTE.
01 / THE CLUB

The hot tub

When the sun goes down, a six-person hot tub with views of the Arizona sky, the outdoor patio TV and Bogey’s Oasis.

02 / THE CLUB

Multi-sport court

Pickleball, basketball and volleyball. The net adjusts from pickleball height up to 17 ft. All equipment lives in the white locker near the patio doors; please return it there.

03 / THE CLUB

Outdoor TV & dining

An outdoor dining table and chairs with non-breakable tableware, plus sun loungers and an outdoor sofa set with cushions. Plastic tableware outdoors, please.

04 / THE CLUB

The grill

An outdoor grill for your cookouts. Please leave it clean. Need another propane tank? One can be delivered on request.

05 / THE CLUB

Mini pickleball & TV

Not your average ping pong table: a mini replica of our outdoor pickleball court, with a TV dedicated to the space. Plus a game station with games for all group sizes and ages.

06 / THE CLUB

Indoor putting green

Get in some practice before hitting The Fore at Sonoran, with our signature mural as the backdrop.

07 / THE CLUB

The outdoor bar

Sip poolside at the custom-built bar. Bar stools stay at the bar; plastic cups or cans only. No glass, please.

08 / THE CLUB

Recreational turf

The open turf near the fire pit is made for lawn games, sunbathing and anything suited to grass. Course spectators welcome.

09 / THE CLUB

Patio sound system

Connect any device by Bluetooth to the two patio speakers, one above the patio furniture and one above the grill. Please keep the volume considerate, especially early and late, and never at full volume.

BEYOND THE BACKYARD

Golfing close by

Warm up on The Fore, then take your game beyond the backyard. Book directly with each course.

Grand Canyon University Golf Course

Phoenix · 18-hole municipal

City-owned former Maryvale Golf Course, operated by GCU. A parkland layout with mature trees and shade; welcomes golfers of all levels.

Book a tee time ›

Bellair Golf Park

Glendale · 18-hole short course

A renovated par-59 executive course that plays in about 2–3 hours, plus a Toptracer range — easy for mixed-ability groups.

Course & tee times ›

Desert Mirage at Golf Ranch

Glendale · 9-hole executive

A relaxed 9-hole executive course alongside a Trackman driving range. Good for a quick round or short-game practice.

Course & tee times ›

Coyote Lakes Golf Club

Surprise · 18 holes

Open for public tee times; known locally as a fun, challenging West Valley course.

Visit course site ›

Rates, hours and availability are set by each course; check their site before you go. Sonoran Country Club is not affiliated with these courses.
