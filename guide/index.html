iiArrival checklist

Arrival & connection

Check-in time

Check-in is at 4:00 PM, Arizona time.

Access code & self check-in

We’ll provide you with an access key code before your arrival so you can check yourself into the property. Please make sure you have received the code before you travel. If you haven’t received it before taking off, please contact us — there’s nothing worse than arriving somewhere new, potentially at night, and not being able to get in.

Your code and arrival details are sent to you securely; they are never shown in this guide.

Early check-in

We may be able to offer an early check-in for a fee. Please contact us if you plan to arrive early and we’ll do our best to accommodate you — we can’t promise, unfortunately.

Contacting us

If you need to get in touch — whether it’s about a delay, you’re lost, or anything else — you can call, text, or message us. Contact details are provided with your reservation.

Wi-Fi & connection help

Free Wi-Fi runs throughout the home. You’ll find the network name and password on the Wi-Fi poster and card inside the home.

Please use it safely and lawfully. If the connection drops, message your host through your booking platform and we’ll sort it out.

Don’t forget to bring

We provide a lot, but you may want to bring:

  • Specialized or personal toiletries such as creams, lotions and sprays. We provide shampoo, conditioner and body wash, and there are two blow dryers.
  • Camera and memory cards.
  • Personal electronics chargers and adaptors as needed. Every nightstand has a MagSafe 3-in-1 charging station.
Directions & getting a ride

The exact street address is shared after your booking is confirmed, along with arrival details. The home is in Glendale, Arizona, in the Phoenix West Valley.

For area weather before you travel, use the Glendale forecast above. Ride and map links with the full address go out in your confirmation materials.

iiiHow things work

Using the indoor equipment

Kitchen equipment

All devices should be fairly straightforward to use. There is a QR code on the dishwasher for easy program settings and basic troubleshooting. If that doesn’t help, contact us.

Heating & cooling

You’ll find the thermostat in the dining room, behind the coffee bar. Please make sure all windows and outside doors are closed so the system operates at maximum efficiency. We are able to control temperature settings remotely — if you need help with the temperature, please contact us.

Laundry

The washer and dryer are in the room directly off the garage entrance. Follow the instructions posted on each machine. If basic troubleshooting doesn’t solve a small issue, please contact us.

Television

You’re welcome to use the TVs and entertainment systems for your personal use. The remote for each TV is in its designated holder near the light switches for that room, and each controller is labeled.

If you log into any streaming services, please log out before you check out.

ivUse it well

Outdoor equipment & care

The Fore at Sonoran

Please be mindful of the greens, flags and putting equipment. All sporting equipment is in the white locker by the patio. Please use the course for golf and putting only.

Multi-sport court

Court sports: pickleball, basketball and volleyball. All equipment is in the white locker near the patio doors — please return everything to the locker when not in use. The net adjusts from pickleball height up to 17 ft.

Pool

The pool is always heated, at no extra charge.

Children 12 and under must always be accompanied by an adult. Swim at your own risk; there is no lifeguard on duty.

Use of the hot tub, pool, fire pit and grill is subject to the risk and liability waiver. No glassware in outdoor spaces.

Fire pit

The fire pit runs on propane, so there’s no need to mess with any fire starters. Please use extra caution when operating it, as an open flame can be dangerous, and keep a safe distance when sitting around the fire.

Grill / BBQ

There is an outdoor grill available for your use. Please leave the grill clean to avoid any additional cleaning fees. If you need another propane tank, we can have one delivered on request.

Outdoor bar

Please leave the bar stools at the bar and use plastic cups and/or canned beverages. No glass, please!

Outdoor tableware & cushions

Please always use the plastic tableware for outdoor dining and entertaining. Please leave the cushions on their designated chairs.

Patio sound system

You’re welcome to use the outdoor sound system and connect any device via Bluetooth.

  1. Find the remote labeled for the patio sound system, inside the patio door.
  2. Turn each speaker on separately: speaker 1 is above the patio furniture, speaker 2 is above the grill.
  3. Once both speakers are on, pair your device with Sync Speaker.

Please be mindful of volume, especially early in the morning and late at night, and never turn music up to full volume.

Looking around the home? Explore Inside — living spaces or Explore Outside — outdoor amenities. For hot-tub operation or a problem not covered here, contact your host through your reservation.

vHouse policies

House rules

  • Quiet hoursWe’re in a friendly, welcoming community — and while this is a vacation for you, it’s home to others. Please respect their privacy and peace: between 11:00 PM and 7:00 AM, keep noise to a minimum.
  • Smoke-freeThis is a smoke-free home, including the outside area surrounding the property. No smoking of any substance, including vaping.
  • Pet-freeWe love animals, but we chose to make this home pet-free because many people are allergic. Please be respectful of that.
  • BreakagesAccidents happen! If you break anything, please let us know immediately. Larger or more expensive items are handled under the rental agreement signed before check-in.

Policies & parking

  • ParkingThere are 2 driveway spaces and ample street parking. Please keep the sidewalk and the neighbors’ driveways clear.
  • Security camerasThere are 3 exterior security cameras: front door entry, garage/driveway, and backyard/pool area. There are no cameras inside the home.
  • No parties or eventsThe club is for an easy-paced stay with your own group, so parties and events aren’t permitted.
  • Pool safetyThe pool is always heated, at no extra charge. Children 12 and under must always be accompanied by an adult. Swim at your own risk; there is no lifeguard on duty. Use of the hot tub, pool, fire pit and grill is subject to the risk and liability waiver. No glassware in outdoor spaces.
viHelp when needed

Troubleshooting & help

Emergency: call 911

For ambulance, police or fire. Do not delay if you feel an emergency situation has arisen. Give the property address from your reservation details.

In case of fire
  1. Exit the property as quickly as possible.
  2. Make sure everyone is accounted for.
  3. Do not re-enter the building until a fire officer says it is safe.
First aid & fire extinguisher

A first-aid kit and fire extinguisher are under the kitchen sink.

Maintenance & housekeeping

For maintenance or housekeeping support, use the contact details provided with your reservation. Those lines are available 8 AM–6 PM.

For anything else, message your host through your booking platform.

viiBefore you leave

Checkout

Checkout is strictly 10:00 AM, Arizona time. If you need a later checkout, we’ll do our best to accommodate it — please let us know beforehand. If guests are arriving that day, a late checkout is almost impossible because the cleaners need to prepare for them.

Required before you leave

Please complete these before checkout.

  • Treat the property with respect
  • Lock all doors and windows
  • Close all propane tanks
  • Turn off the lights before leaving

Optional, but appreciated

Not required. Every little bit helps our team get the club ready for the next guests.

  • Leave beds unmade
  • Put used towels in the shower
  • Gather and rinse dishes in the sink
  • Empty all the trash

Thank you for staying with us — we hope you enjoyed your stay and look forward to seeing you again! If there were any issues, please don’t hesitate to tell us so we can resolve them for the next guest and your future return. We love online reviews. Safe travels home!
