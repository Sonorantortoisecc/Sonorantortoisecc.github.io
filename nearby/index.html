Westside Blues, Jazz & More

North Glendale · 59th Avenue and Bell Road · ticketed live music

Give the outdoor TV the night off. This woman-owned music lounge was PHOENIX magazine’s 2026 Best Jazz Joint in the West Valley, with five shows a week and a dance floor.

Plan the evening: Check the band calendar for blues and jazz, or a tribute show. Dinner at Arrowhead Grill can come first; match your reservation to the actual showtime.

Read before booking: Ticketed shows only, not general bar hours. There is a two-drink minimum per guest; soft drinks count. A 20% gratuity is added to checks, guests under 21 may stay until 10 pm, and ticket sales are final.

Glendale Glitters

Historic Downtown Glendale · seasonal holiday lights

The downtown walk gets its own lighting department. Glendale’s 33-year holiday tradition includes Murphy Park and the Catlin Court district.

2026–27 season: The merchants’ association lists the lighting ceremony on November 27, 2026, with kickoff November 27–28, 5–10 pm. Lights run nightly 5–10 pm through January 1, 2027.

Make a night of it: Browse the downtown shops, then walk the lights. Check the official downtown calendar before going.

Venue site & current details ↗

LiVE! @ Murphy Park

Historic Downtown Glendale · scheduled outdoor concerts

The park supplies the stage; bring a chair or blanket. These concerts are scheduled events, not a promise of music every Saturday.

On the city’s schedule: December 5, 2026: Mega Ran Presents: Buddy’s Magic Holiday; December 12: A Winter’s Evening with Ryanhood; December 19: New Destiny. Each is listed at 7 pm, with free admission.

Confirm first: The city’s October 27 Glitters schedule marks these dates “subject to change.” Its LiVE! page still says to stay tuned for updated dates; check the downtown calendar before planning around a concert.

Venue site & current details ↗

Stir Crazy Comedy Club

Glendale · Westgate Entertainment District · stand-up comedy

Trade the sports-bar commentary for someone with a microphone. Stir Crazy is an intimate Westgate comedy room; its owner told the Glendale Star that weekend shows usually feature touring headliners.

Pick the show, not just the venue: There is a weekly open mic, and some nights are billed as clean, all-ages comedy. That does not make every show family-friendly. Check the current lineup and admission terms.

Venue site & current details ↗

Zero Proof Lounge

Glendale · Olive Avenue · alcohol-free lounge

A bar plan that doesn’t require a designated drinker. This women-owned, speakeasy-style lounge serves botanical mocktails and kava; PHOENIX magazine named it the Valley’s Best Sober Space in 2026.

Beyond the glass: Open mics and game nights give the evening something to revolve around besides alcohol.

Check before leaving: The official site does not list hours. Confirm current opening times and events through its channels.

Venue site & current details ↗

Arizona Broadway Theatre

Peoria · dinner theatre

Dinner has a curtain time here. The theatre has staged full musical productions since 2005, with table service at your seat.

Listed productions: Miracle on 34th Street, November 20–December 27, 2026; My Fair Lady, January 15–February 21, 2027.

Ticket note: The theatre lists tickets starting at $67. It is in Peoria, not Glendale; check the production’s dates and what your ticket includes.

Venue site & current details ↗

Cerreta Candy Company factory tour

Glendale · Glendale Avenue · working chocolate factory

The strongest argument for a weekday sweet tooth. Take a guided tour of the family chocolate factory, with samples along the way.

Choose a pass: The $7 Chocolate Tasting Tour Pass includes three samples. VIP passes ($11.50–$16) add a hands-on project such as a taffy pull or making a chocolate bar.

Weekdays only: Tours run Monday–Friday at 10 am, 11 am, noon, 1 pm and 2 pm. There are no weekend tours; check the current tour page before heading over.

Where we'd eat

Menu prices and provider ratings were checked November 7, 2026 and may change. All listed times are Arizona time (MST). Ratings are shown only for Arrowhead Grill.

Arrowhead Grill

North Glendale · Arrowhead-area steakhouse

The date-night table, with a sizzling plate to announce dinner. This chef-owned steakhouse holds a Wine Spectator Award of Excellence dating to 2014.

Our order: Rhode Island-style calamari ($19.99), named the Valley’s best by PHOENIX magazine in 2026, then the house Delmonico: 10 oz $49.99 or 14 oz $69.99.

Verified ratings: Tripadvisor 4.5/5 (707 reviews), #1 of 640 Glendale restaurants and Travelers’ Choice; OpenTable 4.9/5 (10,073 diner reviews). Checked November 7, 2026.

Before dinner: OpenTable lists smart casual dress and required reservations. The dining room opens at 4 pm daily.

Venue site & current details ↗

Ta'Carbon

Glendale · Camelback Road at 59th Avenue · Mexican counter

The charcoal grill is the point. Expect carne asada at a loud counter with a self-serve salsa bar. USA TODAY named this Glendale location a 2025 Restaurant of the Year; The Infatuation includes its burrito among America’s best.

Our order: The hazz, with charred steak, roasted green chile and melted cheese. Choose a burrito, taco or plate.

Know which door: This is the Glendale location on Camelback Road, not the separate Phoenix or Peoria branches. Counter service; leave the dressy dinner plans for another night.

Venue site & current details ↗

Cultura Mexicana

Glendale · near Historic Downtown · Mexican breakfast and lunch

The murals have competition from the plates. Phoenix New Times put this regional Mexican restaurant on its 2026 Top 50 list, and reports weekend-morning waits.

Our order: The pambazo ($13.99), a chile-dipped roll with chorizo and potato, or the tecolota ($14.49), packed with chilaquiles, meat and eggs. Tres leches French toast is $13.99; cafe de olla belongs on the table.

Brunch planning: The menu lists a 7:30 am opening daily. Closing is 2:30 pm Monday–Thursday, 8 pm Friday–Saturday and 4 pm Sunday. Check the current menu before choosing a late lunch.

Venue site & current details ↗

Happy Bao's

North Glendale · Union Hills Drive · Northern Chinese dumplings

Bring someone who’ll share the dumplings, or order accordingly. The Glendale restaurant uses Northern Chinese family recipes and appears on Phoenix New Times’ 2026 Top 50 list.

Our order: Start negotiations over the 20-piece dumpling combo. Xiao long bao and Dan Dan noodles are also called out by New Times.

At the table: Everything is made to order. The restaurant takes reservations and lists daily hours of 11 am–9 pm. Check its menu for today’s choices.

Venue site & current details ↗

Casa de Falafel

Glendale · Thunderbird Road near ASU West · Middle Eastern counter

Give the falafel the main role. PHOENIX magazine calls it “tender and fluffy” and names it a Top 100 Dishes in Arizona honoree; the family-run counter makes it fresh daily.

Our order: The falafel wrap with lentil soup, following PHOENIX’s recommendation. Shawarma is another option.

For the group: The official site lists vegan and halal options, with high chairs available. Dine in or order takeout back to the house; daily hours are 11 am–8 pm.

Venue site & current details ↗

Haus Murphy's

Historic Downtown Glendale · German restaurant

Sauerbraten before a downtown walk is a defensible dinner plan. This German restaurant is a PHOENIX magazine West Valley pick, with a menu that takes its pretzels seriously.

Our order: Traditional sauerbraten ($29.95), marinated nine days to two weeks and cooked for 12 hours, per the menu. The 10 oz Giant Oktoberfest pretzel is $11.95.

Regular schedule: Closed Monday and Tuesday; Wednesday–Thursday 4–9 pm, Friday–Saturday 11 am–9 pm and Sunday 11 am–8 pm. Reservations are available on the official site.

Temporary notice, checked November 7, 2026: The official homepage says it is closed November 7–8, 2026 and will resume normal business hours November 11. Check its latest notice before making dinner plans.
