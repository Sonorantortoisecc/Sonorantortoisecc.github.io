BEYOND THE BACKYARD

Golfing close by

Warm up on The Fore, then take your game beyond the backyard. Book directly with each course.

Grand Canyon University Golf Course

Phoenix · 18-hole municipal

City-owned former Maryvale Golf Course, operated by GCU. A parkland layout with mature trees and shade; welcomes golfers of all levels.

Book a tee time ›

Bellair Golf Park

Glendale · 18-hole short course

A renovated par-59 executive course that plays in about 2–3 hours, plus a Toptracer range — easy for mixed-ability groups.

Course & tee times ›

Desert Mirage at Golf Ranch

Glendale · 9-hole executive

A relaxed 9-hole executive course alongside a Trackman driving range. Good for a quick round or short-game practice.

Course & tee times ›

Coyote Lakes Golf Club

Surprise · 18 holes

Open for public tee times; known locally as a fun, challenging West Valley course.
